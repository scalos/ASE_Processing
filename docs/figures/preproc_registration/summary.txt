=============== MAIN TAKEAWAYS ===============

OVERALL: The new preprocessing places the ASE data on the anatomy within 0.2 mm of the old (max over the brain); where they differ (echo-2 motion), the new pipeline removes an error in the old.

Fig 1 (reg1_conversion): Dimon (old) and dcm2niix (new) write identical pixels: max |difference| = 0 over all frames (echo 2: 0). Only the header position differs, by [-2.8 0.1 5.7] mm (R, A, S).
Fig 2 (reg2_displacement): Each pipeline's registration cancels its own header offset. For every brain voxel, old and new point to raw ASE positions 0.07 mm apart (median; 95th 0.14, max 0.21 mm) against a 1.72 x 1.72 x 4.8 mm ASE voxel.
Fig 3 (reg3_overlay): The old- and new-registered ASE images are indistinguishable on the anatomy (r = 0.9998 within the brain); white-matter and ventricle contours fall in the same place.
Fig 4 (reg4_roi_dice): ROI masks differ between pipelines less than they would if the segmentation were nudged by 1 mm, in 95% of regions (median Dice old vs new 0.88; 1 mm shift 0.81). Small regions score lower because they are mostly boundary. The remaining difference is likely the 1 mm nearest-neighbour rounding in the ROI method, not registration (not yet tested separately).
Fig 5 (reg5_motion): Echo-1 motion parameters are the same in both pipelines (RMS difference <= 0.002 deg / mm). The old pipeline registered echo 2 on its own against the echo-1 base, which put it off the echo-1 motion by up to 0.47 deg pitch and 0.47 mm dS (RMS); the new pipeline applies the echo-1 transforms to echo 2, since both echoes come from the same excitation. Motion-corrected images, old vs new: median RMS difference 0.01% (echo 1), 4.55% (echo 2) of the signal.

CAVEAT (both pipelines, see docs/motion-correction-plan.md): the echo-1 motion estimates follow the dTE order (dTE explains 83% of dP) and the z-shim block shows up to 2.5 deg of apparent pitch, i.e. 3dvolreg partly reads ASE contrast as motion. This does not separate old from new.
NOTE: ROI membership uses the fixed segToAseMembership (2026-11-20); before the fix, ROI masks were shifted about one slice and matched AFNI resampling in only 38% of labelled voxels.
